# Teaching Experience

## My Teaching Philosophy

My teaching practice brings together rhetorical analysis, literary interpretation, research, and multimodal composition. I design learning experiences that encourage students to become critical readers, effective writers, thoughtful researchers, and reflective communicators.

## University of West Florida

<strong>Adjunct Professor | 2024–Present</strong>

- Teach first-year composition, including ENC 1101 and ENC 1102.
- Develop instruction in rhetorical analysis, academic research, source evaluation, and multimodal literacy.
- Design online learning materials, video lectures, and interactive assignments.

## Daytona State College

<strong>Adjunct Professor | 2025–Present</strong>

- Teach Introduction to Literature (LIT 2000).
- Develop thematic approaches to literary study, including apocalypse, speculative futures, and representations of social and ethical questions.
- Guide students in close reading, literary analysis, and applying critical frameworks to texts.
