# Projects

My academic work explores the intersections of literature, rhetoric, technology, and digital culture. This page highlights developing research interests and digital projects connected to my teaching and doctoral studies.

## Digital Humanities Academic Portfolio

- **Project Description:** A digital academic portfolio documenting my teaching experience, research interests, and developing work in Digital Humanities.
- **Technologies Used:** GitHub, GitHub Pages, Markdown, HTML, Jekyll.
- **Purpose:** To develop a public-facing scholarly presence and practice publishing academic materials on the web.

## Digital Narratives and Digital Literacy

- **Project Description:** Developing research interests in digital storytelling, nonlinear narratives, audience engagement, and digital literacy, particularly in online environments such as TikTok.
- **Technologies Used:** Digital platforms, qualitative research methods, and digital research tools.
- **Purpose:** To investigate how digital platforms shape narrative structures, audience participation, and contemporary literacy practices.

## Literature, Adaptation, and the Anthropocene

- **Project Description:** Exploring adaptation studies, environmental humanities, and representations of ecological crisis, including the relationship between Jeff VanderMeer's *Annihilation* and its film adaptation.
- **Technologies Used:** Digital research tools, scholarly databases, and digital humanities methods.
- **Purpose:** To examine how literary and cinematic narratives represent ecological uncertainty, environmental trauma, and human relationships with the nonhuman world.
